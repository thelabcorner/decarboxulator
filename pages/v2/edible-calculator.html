FAQ
How does the Builder work?

The builder lets you add multiple ingredients. For example, you can combine 3.5g of flower (Weight Mode) with 1g of Kief. The calculator sums the active cannabinoids from all rows, applies decarboxylation math, and divides by servings.

What is "Active THC"?

This is the actual psychoactive potential. If you input THCa (raw), the calculator multiplies it by 0.877 to account for the weight lost as CO2 during heating (decarboxylation). 100mg THCa becomes ~87.7mg THC.

Concentration Mode vs Weight Mode?

Weight Mode: Best for raw materials like Flower or Isolate where you know the mass and assume standard potency or have lab results.
Concentration Mode: Best for extracts/oils where you have a specific weight (e.g., 2g) and a label percentage (e.g., 75% THC).
